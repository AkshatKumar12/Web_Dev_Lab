Maths

The quadratic formula is given by:

        x = (-b ± √(b2 - 4ac)) / (2a)

This formula is used to find the roots of a quadratic equation of the form ax + bx + c = 0, where a, b, and c are constants.

Physics

Newton's second law of motion states that:

        F = m × a

where F is the force applied to an object, m is its mass, and a is the acceleration produced.

Chemistry

The ideal gas law is expressed as:

        PV = nRT

where P is the pressure of the gas, V is its volume, n is the number of moles, R is the ideal gas constant, and T is the temperature in Kelvin.
